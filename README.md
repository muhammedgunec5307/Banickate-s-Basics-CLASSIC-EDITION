THIS MOD IS GETTING REMASTERED!
​You saw that correctly, this Baldi's Basics in Education and Learning mod is actually getting itself a remastered version! This mod has aged really bad and you can definitely tell. The remastered version of the mod will include:

Polished textures + a brand new look for Banickate's studios.
Updated artstyle that's hopefully more consistent than the original.
Brand new characters to replace some of the others.
The characters being replaced are the ones that originated from the original Baldi's Basics for lack of originality, and the infamous Nathan Kokanaoi for controversial reasons.​
Updated voicelines for certain characters to better match their personality.
Cleaner HUD so that everything doesn't feel crammed when everything is enabled.
New and updated secret endings, featuring a secret character.
Fresher comedic implementations. It now relies on dead 2022-2023 memes instead of those from 2020 and the forbidden phrase.
Optimized coding! Gone are the times where it relies on Yandere-Dev type code to do the things it needs to do, and the ClockScript has gotten a complete clean-up.
​Teasers of the remastered versions are up on both my YouTube and my Twitter. Hope to see this mod with a much cleaner state than what it is now when the remaster does come out.

Before the events of Map 1, The Brother was in charge of the studio, until he decided to leave the group because he was annoyed by the amount of random noises and chaos there is...  And the crew has to pick a new person to be in charge of the Studio...
